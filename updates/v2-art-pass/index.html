    The bones — what each face tests

      How a round runs (facilitator)
      1. The crew at the wheel rolls. The die face (1–6) picks the framework in play.
      2. Read the distress call aloud. The crew works the framework out loud — name the rung or check that failed.
      3. Reveal the diagnosis and the bug-or-config verdict, then open that framework's broken workflow and check it against its "fixed when" line. Solved it on their own? They sail the number they rolled. Needed a nudge? They sail 1.
      4. The wheel passes to the next crew. First to the chest wins the voyage.

      About this game: Roll to Resolve is the Session 2 game for the PSS Workflows training on Wednesday, November 11, 2026. The die faces are the five frameworks from the PSS Workflows Enablement Planning doc (plus a Kraken "Bug or Config?" face), the distress calls mirror real #support-copilot cases, and each framework links to an intentionally broken Clay workflow (facilitators set these with the Workflows button).
